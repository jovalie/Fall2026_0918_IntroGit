# Story Template
## Part 0 - Character introduction

Bisc is a sentient biscuit sitting on the QCL table looking at the people around him

## Part 1 — The Beginning

Bisc, the biscuit, is sitting in a box of biscuits this afternoon. He is wondering if the people are going to eat him.

## Part 2 — The Challenge

Bisc, sitting on the table, begins to ponder their sentience. "Why am I the only sentient biscut in the world?" Maybe I'm from another planet. Then suddenly Socrates landed on the table with a thud. "who is to say you are sentient?" Socrates asked. Bisc, insulted, then pulled a sword from his belt and preppared for battle. 

## Part 3 — The Ending

In the end, Bisc is happy and has self love. However, just how Bisc was watching the people, the people were watching him, and then they grabbed him and ate him. :( The end...?

Bisc came back to life as a zombie and explained to the people why it hurt him when they ate him.
